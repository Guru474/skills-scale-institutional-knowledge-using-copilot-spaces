# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme’s project management guidance for cross-functional projects. The documents describe a lightweight, iterative approach that prioritizes customer value, clear ownership, evidence-based decisions, and continuous learning.

## Project management process at a glance

OctoAcme moves work through initiation, planning, execution, release, and closure. Initiation validates the business need, desired outcomes, stakeholders, and go/no-go decision. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and identified risks and dependencies. During execution, teams deliver small increments, track work on a project board, review progress and blockers regularly, and apply quality checks throughout development. Release preparation includes meeting acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, drafting release notes, and preparing smoke tests and rollback or mitigation plans; teams verify deployments and communicate outcomes. At closure, retrospectives capture learnings and assign improvement actions for follow-up.

The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, communication, risks, and dependencies. The Product Manager owns outcomes, backlog prioritization, and success measures. Developers build and test increments; QA/testing validates quality and acceptance criteria; stakeholders contribute input and approvals. Regular team and stakeholder updates, risk reviews, demos, and clear escalation paths help keep work aligned and blockers visible.
